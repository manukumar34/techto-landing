FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How does Webbit ensure data privacy and compliance?

We enforce strict enterprise data boundaries. Your proprietary data is never used to train public LLM models. All pipelines adhere to SOC2, GDPR, and HIPAA encryption standards.

Can AI voice agents integrate with our current CRM?

Yes. Our AI voice agents integrate natively with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Monday.com, and custom REST API endpoints for real-time contact logging and scheduling.

What happens if an AI agent encounters a complex query?

We build intelligent fallback logic. If a conversation exceeds confidence thresholds, the AI agent seamlessly transfers the live call or ticket to your human team with full contextual notes.

What is the typical timeline for an AI automation deployment?

Initial feasibility audits and prototype agents are delivered in 10 to 14 business days. Full production deployment and integration typically take 3 to 5 weeks.
